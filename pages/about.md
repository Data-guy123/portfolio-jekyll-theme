# About Me

Hi, I’m Joseph Wright, based in Heathfield, East Sussex, UK. I work in digital systems management, helping to make complex tools easier to use and more effective for project teams.

I recently completed the Professional Certificate in Data Analytics from Imperial College, focusing on practical ways to turn data into insights that actually help people.

My background is in innovation support and project operations, and I’m interested in using data to improve decision-making and outcomes. One of my goals is to keep building my skills and take on projects that have a real-world impact.

Outside of work, I spend my time adventuring, rollerblading, weightlifting, and having fun with my two boys, Isaac and Arlo. They keep life fast-paced and full of imagination.
